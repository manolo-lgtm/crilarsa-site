Our plant in operation

Two large industrial buildings on Vía a la Costa, km 22, with heavy machinery and loading docks, ready to handle large volumes.

Industrial capacity

Tour of the operation

We operate two large industrial buildings on Vía a la Costa, km 22, with heavy machinery and loading docks, plus large areas for raw material, storage and expansion. A plant ready to handle large volumes efficiently. An integrated operation: from the extraction and reception of raw material to the stacking and loading of the finished product.

  • Quarry extraction
  • Finished product in the warehouse
  • Stacking on pallets
  • Loading with forklifts
STAGE 01

Extraction and reception of raw material

Limestone is extracted at the quarry with heavy machinery and arrives at the plant in dump trucks, where it is stored in the raw material yard.

STAGE 02

Feeding and crushing

The mineral enters the feed hopper and is crushed before going to the mills.

STAGE 03

Milling

Our mills reduce the mineral to the required particle size, with cyclones and dust collection systems.

STAGE 04

Classification and internal transport

The product is classified and moved by elevators and internal conveying to the bagging hoppers.

STAGE 05

Bagging

The finished product is bagged and labeled with its net content.

STAGE 06

Stacking and loading

Bags are stacked on pallets in the warehouse and loaded with forklifts, which speeds up the preparation of every shipment.

Operational control

How we care for every batch

  • Particle size control for every product.
  • Technical data sheet for every product.
  • INP registration for Cal Forte, Neutrocal and Zeolite.
  • Products labeled in bags with their net content.
